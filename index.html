    About

    Synopsis

    "When renowned crime novelist Harlan Thrombey (Christopher Plummer) is found dead at his estate just after his 85th birthday, the inquisitive and debonair Detective Benoit Blanc (Daniel Craig) is mysteriously enlisted to investigate. From Harlan's disfunctional family to his devoted staff, Blanc sifts through a web of red herrings and self-serving lies to uncover the truth behind Harlan's untimely death." - Lionsgate

    Writer/Director

    Rian Johnson

    Release Date

    November 27th, 2019

    Characters

    Meet the Thrombeys: where everyone has a motive, everyone has a secret, and no one is above suspicion.

    Victim
    Harlan Thrombey Patriarch of the Thrombey family. Was a highly successful mystery novelist who unexpectedly died the night of his 85th birthday party.
    Children and Spouses
    Linda Drysdale Harlan's eldest daughter. Married to Richard Drysdale. Mother to Hugh Drysdale. Real estate mogul and business owner.
    Richard Drysdale Harlan's son-in-law. Married to Linda Drysdale. Father to Hugh Drysdale. Co-runs Linda's real estate company.
    Walt Thrombey Harlan's youngest son. Married to Donna Thrombey. Father to Jacob Thrombey. CEO of Harlan's book publishing company.
    Donna Thromey Harlan's daughter-in-law. Married to Walt Thrombey. Mother to Jacob Thrombey. Stay-at-home mom.
    Neil Thrombey Harlan's deceased middle son. Joni Thrombey's late husband. Father to Meg Thrombey.
    Joni Thrombey Harlan's widowed daughter-in-law. Mother to Meg Thrombey. Skincare business owner and online lifestyle influencer.
    Grandchildren
    Hugh Drysdale Harlan's eldest grandchild. Son to Richard and Linda Drysdale. Unemployed trust fund baby.
    Jacob Thrombey Harlan's grandson. Son to Walt and Donna Thrombey. High school student.
    Meg Thrombey Harlan's granddaughter. Daughter to Neil and Joni Thrombey. Liberal arts college student.
    Others
    Marta Cabrera Harlan's nurse and personal caregiver. Benoit Blanc's temporary assistant.
    Benoit Blanc World-renowned private investigator and consulting detective. Coordinates with Lieutenant Elliot.
    Lieutenant Elliot Local police detective and lead investigator for the county. Reluctantly allows Benoit Blanc to tag along and lead the questioning.

    Fun Facts

    Think you know Knives Out? Click a photo to flip it and uncover something you didn't know!

    No Trailers Allowed

    Instead of retreating to separate trailers between scenes, the entire all-star ensemble cast hung out together in the mansion's living room. This choice helped them build the chaotic, overlapping chemistry of a highly dysfunctional family.

    A Green Screen Painting

    The oil portrait of Harlan Thrombey was not actually finished when filming began. The crew used a green screen inside the frame for every single scene, and the portrait was added entirely in post-production.

    The Secret Character Voice

    Rian Johnson's long-time collaborator Joseph Gordon-Levitt makes a hidden cameo. He voices "Detective Hardrock," the character on the true-crime television show that Marta's sister watches early in the film.
